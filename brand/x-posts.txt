A technical overview 🧵
=== THREAD 2/6 ===
How a market works:

• The creator sets a question, two sides, a fixed stake and a deadline
• Participants pick a side and send exactly that stake
• The contract closes entries at the deadline
• The creator confirms the result and winners claim their share
=== THREAD 3/6 ===
Payouts are deterministic.

Every stake is held in contract escrow. On settlement, a 0.1% protocol fee is taken (fixed per market at creation, hard-capped at 5%) and each winner claims an equal share of the rest.

Rounding dust joins the fee balance. Nothing is stranded.
=== THREAD 4/6 ===
Safety paths enforced on-chain:

• Not settled within 72h of the deadline: anyone can open refunds
• No one took the other side: refundable at the deadline, no fee
• Payouts are pull-based and claimable once per wallet
• Status only moves forward; results are final
=== THREAD 5/6 ===
Trust model, stated plainly:

The market creator reports the outcome. BOT Chain enforces who may report, when, and how funds move. It cannot verify real-world events.

Join pools created by people you trust.

=== POST 3 ===
What happens if a market creator disappears?

72 hours after the deadline, any wallet can switch the market to refunds. Every participant then claims back exactly what they staked, with no fee.

No admin action or backend is needed to trigger it.
